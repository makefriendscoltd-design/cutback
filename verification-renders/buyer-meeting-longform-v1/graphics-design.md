- Safe placement: x 150, y 330, 520×390. This occupies the slide-photo area and avoids the lecturer at bottom right, the slide text, and the caption baseline near y 1000.
- Visual language: warm white paper, dark navy type, orange signal color, restrained shadow, 24 px corner radius.
- Motion: one card entrance, staged diagram construction, long readable hold, clean exit. Each diagram uses actual nodes, arrows, branching, or checked steps rather than a text-only reveal.
- Typography: local Pretendard SemiBold. No school branding or added factual claims.
- Source evidence and exact source-time anchors are stored in `graphic-events-source.json`; final cut times are intentionally left for the root assembly mapping.
